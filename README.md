# ☕ Lusso Cafe — A Taste of Luxury

A modern, elegant, single-page website for **Lusso Cafe**, a warm corner for beautifully brewed coffee, delicious little treats and conversations worth slowing down for.

> *Good coffee. Beautiful moments.*

---

## ✨ Features

| # | Section | What it does |
|---|---------|--------------|
| – | **Hero** | Full-screen coffee imagery with headline and two CTAs: *Explore Our Menu* and *Reserve Your Table* |
| 01 | **Our Story** | Brand story with image and "Discover Our World" link |
| 02 | **Our Menu** | "Shop by category" cards: Coffee, Drinks, Desserts, Bites |
| – | **Best Sellers** | Carousel of customer favourites with rank badges, prices and ratings |
| – | **Full Menu** | Filterable menu (All Items / Coffee / Drinks / Desserts / Bites) with Bestseller tags |
| – | **Most Loved Dishes** | Dish cards with a clickable ❤ heart and "people love this" counts |
| 03 | **The Art of Coffee** | Quality Ingredients, Skilled Hands, Beautiful Moments |
| 04 | **The Lusso Experience** | Good Company, Quiet Corners, Special Gatherings |
| 05 | **Why Lusso Cafe** | Freshly Prepared, Warm Hospitality, Made for Moments |
| 06 | **A Little Something Extra** | Offers section with "Plan Your Visit" CTA and scrolling marquee |
| 07 | **Celebrate at Lusso** | Small Celebrations and Group Meetups |
| 08 | **The Gallery** | Photo gallery: "Little moments. Lasting memories." |
| 09 | **Kind Words** | Guest reviews with 5-star ratings |
| 10 | **Reservation Form** | Name, phone, date, time, guests and special request |
| – | **FAQ** | Accordion-style frequently asked questions |
| – | **Footer** | Quick links, contact details and social links |
| – | **Ask Lusso** | Floating chatbot that answers questions about the menu, best sellers and more |

### UI extras
- 📊 Scroll progress indicator (header bar and circular percentage badge)
- ⬆️ Back-to-top button
- 🧭 Sticky navigation with smooth-scroll anchors
- 📱 Responsive layout for laptop, tablet and mobile
- 💾 Reservation requests are saved in the browser (localStorage)

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Option 1: VS Code Live Server (recommended)
1. Open the project folder in **VS Code**.
2. Install the **Live Server** extension.
3. Right-click `index.html` and choose **Open with Live Server**.
4. The site opens at `http://127.0.0.1:5500/index.html`.

### Option 2: Open directly
Double-click `index.html` to open it in your browser.

## 🤖 Ask Lusso Chatbot

The floating **Ask Lusso** button opens a chat window where visitors can ask about:
- Menu categories and best sellers
- Prices
- Reservations and contact details

---

## 📅 Table Reservations

The booking form collects:
- Name and phone number
- Date and time
- Number of guests
- Optional special request

> **Note:** Requests are currently saved in the visitor's browser only. Customers should call the café to confirm table availability. To receive bookings, connect the form to a backend, email service (e.g. EmailJS, Formspree) or Google Sheets.
